I am a Research Scientist in Machine Perception at ABB Corporate Research Switzerland, where I have been working since September 2026.

Previously, I conducted my doctoral research at École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) under the supervision of Prof. Michael Unser. I completed my private PhD defense with the thesis Principled Image Reconstruction with Adaptive Regularizers and Generative Priors.

I also interned at Disney Research Studios in Zürich. I hold a BSc in Electrical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology (SUT).

Research Background

My doctoral research at EPFL focused on image reconstruction and the design of image priors, spanning sparse regularization, deep learning, and generative models based on flow matching.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in machine perception, computational imaging, and generative modeling, particularly diffusion and flow matching. I am interested in how mathematical structure and domain knowledge can guide learning from complex data.
